Why degree ≤ 3 has no solution at cell (21, 27)

Two required pairs need straight track through the same cell, one horizontal and one vertical. With no 4-way tile, one of them has to bend. Bending costs Marseille → Mons two extra tiles.

Stuttgart → Paris (only shortest path) Marseille → Mons Dijon → Mons Cloud (blocked) Port City

What would fix it

  • Crossing tile: allow degree 4 only as straight-through in both directions, with no turns.
  • 4-way junction: allow degree 4 with any turns.
  • Allow excess length: keep degree ≤ 3 and accept the +2 jog, minimising excess length before tiles.
  • Accept infeasibility: keep the rules, and the real map becomes a "no" instance.
